# Welcome to Syhunt

Thank you for choosing Syhunt. Our goal is to help you get the most out of the Syhunt tools so your organization can reach stronger application security and compliance faster, by finding and fixing vulnerabilities and weaknesses before they reach production.

Start with the four steps below, then read the rest of this guide.

## Initial steps

### 1. Activate your license

![](../images/icon_unlock.webp)

**If you haven't activated your license yet, do it now so you can start using the Syhunt tools.**

1. Download the setup executable from the link provided by Syhunt or your Syhunt Authorized Partner.
2. Double-click the downloaded file and follow the installer (a standard *Next → Next → Finish* process). Syhunt launches when you click **Finish**.
3. Enter your Product Key File, either by following the instructions in the email, or by [enabling the Web UI](../installation/web-ui.md#firststeps), which prompts you for it. Use the key file attached to the email that contained your download link.
4. Click **Apply**. A success message confirms that Syhunt is ready, and the available options become enabled on the Home screen.

If Syhunt doesn't ask for a key, a license is already active and must be replaced. To replace it, open `https://127.0.0.1:8018/syhunt/?n=license` or run again the CLI command you used to activate your key.

### 2. Subscribe to notifications

![](../images/icon_notification.webp)

**Subscribe to the Customer Newsletter** to receive an email whenever product updates and enhancements become available. Updates can be downloaded and installed directly from the Syhunt website. Syhunt 6.8.4 and later also announce new releases in the Launcher and in reports.

- [Subscribe here](https://www.syhunt.com/en/?n=Syhunt.SubscribeForNotifications)

### 3. Follow the POC test plan

![](../images/icon_check.webp)

**We strongly recommend working through the [Syhunt Proof-of-Concept Test Plan](poc-test-plan.md).** It walks you through scans under a variety of conditions that simulate real-world use. By the end, you will be familiar with the key product features and integration options.

- [Test plan (Markdown)](poc-test-plan.md)
- [Test plan (PDF)](https://www.syhunt.com/docs/SyhuntHybrid_POCTestPlan.pdf)

### 4. Read the quick start guides

![](../images/icon_docs.webp)

**Set aside time for you and your technical staff to read the quick start guides** and the other documents in this repository, and get familiar with the tools and features your organization plans to use.

- [Documentation index](../README.md)
- [Syhunt Support area](https://www.syhunt.com/en/?n=Syhunt.Support)

## Getting help

![](../images/icon_support.webp)

The [Syhunt Support area](https://www.syhunt.com/en/?n=Syhunt.Support) is the fastest way to find answers. If you have a question or run into a problem, [open a support ticket](../support/reporting-bugs.md#supportticketcreation). When reporting a false positive or any other issue, follow the [bug reporting guidelines](../support/reporting-bugs.md) for a faster resolution.

### Requesting features

You can help shape the product roadmap. Send feature and improvement requests to our product management team at any time by opening a support ticket.

### Viewing and renewing your license

Syhunt or your Syhunt Authorized Partner will contact you about 90 days before your license ends so you can renew your license or support coverage. Syhunt 6.7.1 and later also show a reminder in the main interface about 60 days before the end date. For questions about this process or about additional requirements, contact Syhunt or your Syhunt Authorized Partner.

You can check your license term and details at any time from **Help → License**.

### Training

Training may be available in your region and language. Contact your Syhunt Authorized Partner to check availability, fees and schedules.
