# Download

Syhunt installers are distributed through the [Syhunt downloads page](https://www.syhunt.com/en/?n=Docs.SyhuntHybridDownload).

| Edition | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Syhunt Hybrid** and **Syhunt Runner** | Use the download link and customer key sent to you by Syhunt or your Syhunt Authorized Partner. |
| **Demo / trial** | Use the trial download link you received after requesting a demo. |
| **Syhunt Community** | Available on the downloads page without a key. |
| **Prey Server** (vulnerable test applications) | See [Prey Server](poc-test-plan.md#preyserver) in the POC test plan. |

If your license has expired, [contact Syhunt](https://www.syhunt.com/en/?n=Syhunt.ContactUs) to renew it.

Once you have the installer, follow the guide for your platform:

- [Windows](../installation/install-windows.md)
- [Linux](../installation/install-linux.md)
- [macOS](../installation/install-macos.md)
- [Wine (Linux and macOS)](../installation/wine.md)
